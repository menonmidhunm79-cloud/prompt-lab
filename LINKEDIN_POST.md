I built a tool that grades your AI prompts, then rewrites them better. ⚡

Meet PROMPT·LAB, a Prompt Evaluation Studio that runs entirely in Google Colab.

You write a prompt. An AI judge scores it on six pillars:
Role · Goal · Context · Constraints · Format · Style
...then gives feedback, a radar chart, and an optimized version. You can even battle-test your prompt against the improved one, side by side, and watch your score climb attempt after attempt.

What I enjoyed building:
🧬 An electro-biomorph UI (charcoal, forest green, crimson) with no Streamlit, just Python + Flask + vanilla JS
✍️ A spring-physics typing caret and cursor-reactive typography
🧠 LLM-as-a-judge with strict JSON-schema output and automatic fallback
📈 Live scoring, history, and exportable reports
